# Logo di Topperia

Creato il **28 ottobre 2026** con **ImageGen integrato**, su richiesta dell’utente. Il simbolo combina una T, carte da trivia sovrapposte e una scintilla di scoperta. Verde bosco, lime e lilla riprendono l’identità del gioco.

## Asset e integrazione

- `topperia-logo.png`: originale trasparente, 1254 × 1254 pixel, RGBA. Non ritoccato dopo la generazione.
- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/topperia_logo.png`: copia identica usata dall’app.
- Il launcher Android usa icone adattive con sfondo avorio e margine per le maschere circolari o arrotondate.
- Lo stesso simbolo compare nella schermata di avvio Android, nelle intestazioni del gioco e nella prima scena animata dell’onboarding.
- Il nome «topperia» accanto al simbolo è testo nativo Android, leggibile a ogni densità.

## Prompt definitivo

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a production-quality original standalone app logomark for TOPPERIA, a joyful Italian Top 10 trivia game. The identity must be striking and ownable at tiny Android app-icon sizes, with an exceptionally clean professional silhouette, like a carefully designed contemporary mobile game brand.

Design one unified emblem: a bold friendly rounded capital T that also reads as a little winning podium made of overlapping quiz cards, rising upward. Strong dark forest green main structure (#183B32 / #315D43), a bright lime inset or card face (#DCF58D), a restrained lavender offset card accent (#DDD3F5), and a small four-point discovery sparkle integrated near the upper right. Make the T/podium geometry clever, visually balanced, dimensional only through subtle overlapping flat colored layers. Confident thick forms, smooth precise rounded corners, a small amount of negative space, no thin detail. The symbol should evoke discovery, ranking and reaching the top, rather than banking or bar-chart software.

Asset: a SINGLE centered standalone emblem on a genuinely transparent background, square 1024x1024 canvas, emblem occupies about 76% of the canvas with clean generous transparent margins. No enclosing app tile, no enclosing circle, no exterior cast shadow, no scene, no mockup. Extremely crisp polished edges. High-end playful flat graphic design, restrained subtle depth only at card overlaps, no photorealism, no texture. This exact mark will appear in both Android launcher icons and inside the warm ivory game UI.

Text: NO lettering, NO wordmark, NO numerals, NO labels other than the inherently geometric T-like symbol. No extra objects, no watermarks, no brand sheet, no multiple variants.
```
